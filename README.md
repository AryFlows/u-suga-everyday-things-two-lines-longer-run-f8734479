## The entry

> A toaster is a tiny sun trapped in a box that gives your bread a tan.
> It maps how heat browns bread the way sun browns skin, but it leaves out that the real sun takes hours, never pops your toast up, and has no lever to stop it.

Two lines, 228 characters. Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old through one concrete, surprising analogy — a sun in a box giving bread a tan. Line 2 says honestly what the analogy maps (heat browning bread like sun browning skin) and what it leaves out (the time scale, the pop-up, the lever).
